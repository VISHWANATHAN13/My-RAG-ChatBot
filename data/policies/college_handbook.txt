College Handbook

Attendance Policy:
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% to be eligible to appear for semester examinations.

Leave Policy:
Students who require leave must submit a leave request to their class advisor. Medical leave requests should include supporting medical documents.

Examination Policy:
Students must carry their college identity card and examination hall ticket to the examination hall.

Library Policy:
Students may borrow up to three books at a time. Books must be returned within 14 days of borrowing.
